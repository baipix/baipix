## The Move tool

With the Move tool (<kbd>V</kbd>), the layer you'll move is framed, with handles around its pixels.

- **Click a pixel** to grab its layer, and drag. Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> to move the active layer wherever you click.
- **Arrow keys** nudge it by 1 pixel, with <kbd>Shift</kbd> by 8.
- **Click an empty spot**, or press <kbd>Escape</kbd>, to let go of the frame.
- A layer dragged out of every board stays there, beside them: see [Boards](../../boards/boards/).

## Resize and rotate

- Drag a **corner** or a **side** of the frame to resize, with sharp pixels. <kbd>Shift</kbd> keeps the proportions, <kbd>Alt</kbd> resizes from the center.
- Just outside a corner, the cursor turns: drag to **rotate**.
- Several selected layers resize and turn together, as one.

## Flip, turn, align

In the Image menu, for the selection or the layer:

- **Flip horizontally** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>) and **vertically** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd>).
- **Rotate 90°** clockwise (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd>) or counterclockwise (<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd>).
- **Align** what's drawn to the board: left, center, right, top, middle or bottom (<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd>, <kbd>H</kbd>, <kbd>D</kbd>, <kbd>W</kbd>, <kbd>V</kbd>, <kbd>S</kbd>).
