Three tools share a button: the **rectangle** (<kbd>M</kbd>), the **lasso** (<kbd>Q</kbd>), which follows your hand and closes when you let go, and the **magic wand** (<kbd>Y</kbd>), which selects a color's area, or that color everywhere with **Touching area only** off.

- <kbd>Shift</kbd> adds to the selection, <kbd>Alt</kbd> takes from it.
- **Select › Select all**, **Invert selection** and **Deselect** are in the Select menu.

## Use it

- Every tool keeps inside the selection: paint, fill, blur or erase without touching the rest.
- Drag it to move what's selected. The arrow keys nudge it by 1 pixel, with Shift by 8.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd>, <kbd>X</kbd> and <kbd>V</kbd> copy, cut and paste. A paste lands as a new layer.
- <kbd>Del</kbd> clears it, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Del</kbd> fills it with the color (with nothing selected, the whole layer).
- Flip and rotate it with the Image menu, or turn it into a brush from the pencil's … menu.
